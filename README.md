# Retail Sales and Profit Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales and profit data to identify sales trends, regional performance, product performance, and customer segment profitability.

The analysis involved cleaning the dataset and analyzing sales and profit data using Excel and Power BI.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales: $14.92M
- Total Profit: $1.52M
- Total Orders: $5,496

## Analysis Performed
- Sales performance by region
- Sales performance by product category
- Top products by sales
- Profit by customer segment
- Monthly sales trends
- Overall sales and profit analysis

## Key Insights
- The West region recorded the highest sales contribution, with approximately $3.60M in sales.
- Technology was the highest-performing product category, generating approximately $5.98M in sales and contributing 58.24% of total profit.
- The Global Troyª Executive Leather Low-Back Tilter recorded the highest sales among the analyzed products, at approximately $275,942.
- The Corporate customer segment generated the highest profit, contributing approximately $599,746.
- September 2010 recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately $463,269.
